# Decisions Log

Records decisions that shape the Expert Hub build. Two kinds:
- **🔒 Locked by BRD** — already decided in `BRD-TRN-001 v1.0`; do not re-litigate.
- **🟢 Project decision** — made by the team during design/build (add as they happen).

Format: `ID · date · decision · rationale · source`
