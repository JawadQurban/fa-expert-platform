*Prepared 2026-09-17 on `expert-hub/uat-baseline`. Every row is a question for the
business. **None of them is decided here.** "Current safe behavior" is what the
code does today; "Recommended technical default" is only what engineering would
build if asked, and it doesn't change until the owner approves it.*

Status values: `OPEN` (not raised yet) · `RAISED` (with the owner) · `APPROVED` ·
`REJECTED`.

| BD-UAT-03 | J-10 / J-11 Agreement signing | Is the internal in-platform acceptance legally sufficient, or is an external e-signature provider required? | Acceptance is recorded as method `internal-acceptance` against the exact frozen document version and its SHA-256 hash. | BD-UAT-04 | J-11 Agreement activation | Which date does an agreement's term **start** from? | Activation sets `StartsAt` = **the applicant's signature timestamp** and `EndsAt` = that date + the term years (`ApplicantAgreementEndpoints.cs`).
